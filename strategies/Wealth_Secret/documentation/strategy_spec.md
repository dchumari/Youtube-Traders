# Strategy Specification — Wealth Secret
## Version 1.0 | XAUUSD High-Impact Macro Catalyst News Breakout Strategy

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Wealth Secret exploits the extreme directional momentum generated by US high-impact economic releases on XAUUSD. The strategy pre-defines a consolidation range prior to news, waits for the initial post-release spike, then enters on the SECONDARY impulse wave (avoiding the chaotic first spike). This avoids slippage during the release itself while still capturing the sustained directional move. The strategy is purely event-driven, not session-based.

## 2. Event Calendar & Filtering

### 2.1 Qualifying Events
Only trade these Tier-1 US economic releases:
| Event | Frequency | Expected XAUUSD Move |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| US CPI (Consumer Price Index) | Monthly | 50–150+ pips |
| US PPI (Producer Price Index) | Monthly | 30–80 pips |
| US NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls) | Monthly (1st Friday) | 80–200+ pips |
| FOMC Rate Decision | ~8/year | 100–300+ pips |
| US Initial Jobless Claims | Weekly | 20–50 pips |

### 2.2 Deviation Threshold Filter
Trade ONLY if the actual economic result deviates significantly from forecast:
| Event | Minimum Required Deviation |
|-------|--------------------------|
| CPI | ≥ 0.2% vs forecast |
| PPI | ≥ 0.2% vs forecast |
| NFP | ≥ 50,000 jobs vs forecast |
| FOMC | Any surprise in rate decision or forward guidance |

> **EA Implementation**: Deviation data cannot be fetched in real-time in MQL5 without external feed. EA uses manual news time input + user confirmation mode. Alternatively, EA trades ALL qualifying event times without deviation filter (more conservative with spread control).

### 2.3 Directional Bias from Deviation
- **CPI/PPI Higher than forecast** → Hawkish Fed → Stronger USD → XAUUSD **SHORT**
- **CPI/PPI Lower than forecast** → Dovish Fed → Weaker USD → XAUUSD **LONG**
- **NFP Higher than forecast** → USD strength → XAUUSD **SHORT**
- **NFP Lower than forecast** → USD weakness → XAUUSD **LONG**

> EA uses simplified bidirectional approach: observe initial spike direction within 60 seconds of release and trade in that direction on secondary impulse.

## 3. Signal Generation — Pre & Post News Framework

### 3.1 Pre-News Range Construction (T-30 to T-0 minutes)
- 30 minutes before event, mark:
  - **Range High (RH)**: Highest high of the 30-minute consolidation period
  - **Range Low (RL)**: Lowest low of the 30-minute consolidation period
  - **Range Midpoint (RM)**: (RH + RL) / 2
- This range defines the "pre-news equilibrium zone"

### 3.2 Initial Spike Observation (T+0 to T+60 seconds)
- At release, price makes an immediate sharp move
- Record the spike high or spike low within 60 seconds
- **Spike Direction**: If price breaks ABOVE RH → Bullish initial spike; below RL → Bearish
- Do NOT enter during this spike (slippage, spread explosion to 50–200 points)

### 3.3 Secondary Impulse Entry (T+1 to T+10 minutes)
1. Wait for initial spike to exhaust and retrace
2. Retracement must be 20–50% of spike magnitude (shallow = strong momentum remains)
3. On 1M chart: first bullish close after retracement (for long) / first bearish close (for short)
4. Enter in direction of initial spike
5. **Invalidation**: If retracement exceeds 61.8% of spike → trade cancelled

### 3.4 Minimum Spike Size
- Spike must exceed pre-news range by at least 15 pips to qualify
- Too-small spikes = unclear direction / likely re-price of expectations only

## 7. Mathematical Edge Analysis

- **Win Rate Target**: 60–70% (directional moves post-surprise CPI/NFP are sustained)
- **Average R:R**: 2.5 (2× range width with 50% partial close mechanic)
- **Expected Value**: EV = (0.65 × 2.5) − (0.35 × 1) = 1.625 − 0.35 = **+1.28R per trade**
- **Profit Factor Target**: 2.0–3.0
- **Frequency**: 4–8 qualifying events per month
- **Caution**: Outlier risk — on extreme data releases (e.g., CPI+0.6% surprise), spreads can reach 100–200 points; the spread filter protects against this

## 8. Automation Limitations

- Real-time economic calendar integration requires external API (not native in MT5)
- EA relies on pre-programmed news event times (updated monthly) as input parameters
- Deviation threshold filtering is manual — EA trades all pre-programmed events
- **Recommended**: Run EA only on days with scheduled events; disable on non-event days
