## Key Concepts — BST Model

### 3-Layer Framework
1. **Behavior (1H timeframe)**
   - Identify rejection wicks at key Support/Resistance levels
   - Long upper wicks at resistance = bearish behavior; long lower wicks at support = bullish
   - Wick must be at least 50% of the total candle range to qualify

2. **Structure (4H timeframe)**
   - Establish macro directional bias using swing highs/lows
   - Bullish bias: 4H forming higher highs and higher lows
   - Bearish bias: 4H forming lower highs and lower lows
   - Only trade in direction of 4H structure

3. **Trend Entry (15M/5M timeframe)**
   - Wait for price to break a minor structure level and retest
   - Break + retest on 15M aligns with 1H behavior and 4H structure
   - Entry on close of retest candle (pullback entry)

### Confluence Required
- ALL 3 layers must align: Behavior + Structure + Trend
- No 2-layer trades (strict rule)

## Risk Parameters Observed
- **Stop Loss**: Beyond 1H behavior candle wick + 5-10 pip buffer
- **Take Profit**: Next 4H structural swing / liquidity level
- **Risk per Trade**: 0.5–1%

- **Trailing Stop**: Manual trailing after breakeven
