## Key Concepts — SST Methodology

### Multi-Timeframe S/R Framework
1. **Daily/4H Key Levels** — Mark previous day's high/low, weekly open, major swing pivots
2. **Round Numbers** — Psychological levels (e.g., 1900, 1950, 2000 on XAUUSD) act as magnet levels
3. **Candlestick Confirmation** — At key S/R, wait for:
   - **Engulfing Pattern** — Full body engulf of prior candle (bullish/bearish)
   - **Pin Bar / Hammer** — Long wick rejection at S/R zone
   - Pattern must appear on 15M or 1H timeframe
4. **Fundamental Catalyst Alignment** — Check US Treasury yields and Fed outlook; bullish gold = falling yields + dovish Fed

### Entry Criteria
- Price reaches pre-defined Daily/4H S/R level
- 15M engulfing or pin bar forms at level
- Macroeconomic context supports direction (yields/DXY aligned)
- Entry on close of confirmation candle

## Risk Parameters Observed
- **Stop Loss**: 10–15 pips beyond confirmation candle wick
- **Take Profit**: Next Daily/4H S/R level
- **Risk per Trade**: 1–2%

- **Breakeven**: At 1:1 R:R
