# JadeCap_FX — Strategy Specification & MQL5 Logic

## 1. Strategy Overview
This Expert Advisor implements the signature trading models of **Jade (@JadeCapFX)**, built for high-precision institutional execution on Gold (`XAUUSD`) and Nasdaq (`NQ` / `US100`).
